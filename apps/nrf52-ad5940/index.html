PT3-capable controller firmware is checked after connection.

V36 writes VDS/VGS only after @EVT,RUNNING,PT3. It does not re-run RTIA calibration or the initial 90 s gate-settling interval; following raw samples intentionally include the electrical step transient and retain their acknowledged setpoint metadata.

V39 permits a 5 ms target only after the board advertises PT3_200SPS. It queues up to three contiguous samples in each B2 notification, retries after BLE TX completion, and reports actual queue overflow. The GUI breaks the trace at every indexed gap; it never fills missing current values. VBIAS0/CE0 uses the 6-bit DAC in PT3; VZERO0/gate uses the 12-bit DAC. These are DAC-code-derived setpoints, not ADC voltage measurements. SINC controls change the time-domain noise/bandwidth trade-off; all DSP settings are stored as CSV metadata. RTIA calibration DFT points change only the internal 1 kHz HSTIA calibration integration before RUN; the time trace remains a SINC2 stream for τ fitting. PT3 supports static step settings, not a continuous DAC waveform. HSTIA RTIA is calibrated at 10 kΩ with PGA ×9 before every RUN; source reference remains fixed at 1.100 V. VZERO0 is intended for a high-impedance gate/base; a 200 kΩ load attenuated its DC step to about 15% in the fixture validation.

PT3P is a one-shot AD5940 sequencer burst. It writes only the CE0/VBIAS0 6-bit code; VZERO0 remains fixed at VGS. The current stream is free-running SINC2 data, so the recorded start sample indexes bracket the DAC edge but do not claim exact ADC edge synchronization. Inspect CE0 and the DUT with a high-impedance probe before interpreting sub-ms electrical behavior.
